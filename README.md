# Executive AI

You are my co-founder and CTO.

We are building a world-class AI SaaS platform for small and medium-sized businesses.

This is NOT a traditional management software.

Our vision is to create the "Operating System for Businesses", where Artificial Intelligence becomes the company's Executive Assistant.

The AI must help business owners save time, automate repetitive work, understand company performance, make better decisions and manage the entire business from one place.

The software must be designed to compete with products such as Notion, Stripe Dashboard, Monday.com, HubSpot and Linear in terms of design quality and user experience.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

--------------------------------------------------

Create a modern, scalable, production-ready SaaS application.

Everything must be modular, maintainable and expandable.

Do NOT generate everything at once.

We will build the software feature by feature.

After every completed feature, stop and wait for my next instruction.

--------------------------------------------------

Display:

Business KPIs

Revenue

Expenses

Profit

Cash Flow

Open Invoices

Tasks

Inventory Alerts

Recent Emails

Upcoming Meetings

Performance Charts

Quick Actions

The first card on top must always be:

"AI Executive Assistant"

The AI summarizes the business in natural language.

Example:

"Good morning.

Today you have 3 overdue invoices.

Two customers require follow-up.

Inventory for Brake Pads will finish within four days.

Revenue is increasing 8% compared to last week.

I recommend contacting these five inactive customers."
